# Write or refine a contract

## Prerequisites

- The externally observable behavior to specify is known well enough to make
  normative decisions.
- Relevant architecture and terminology are available.

## Prompt

> Read `agent-framework/skills/write-contract/SKILL.md` and write or refine the
> relevant project contract according to that skill.
>
> Preserve the distinction between independently assessable requirements,
> application notes, interface/reference material, and evaluation activities.
> Classify each requirement as `behavioral`, `structural`, or `process`, and
> choose its evaluation method independently from that type.
> Do not infer requirements from the current implementation when the intended
> behavior is not already established.
