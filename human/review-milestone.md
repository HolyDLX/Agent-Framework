# Review a completed milestone

## Prerequisites

- The implementation milestone is complete enough for independent review.
- Its handover and authoritative specifications are available.

## Prompt

> Read `agent-framework/skills/review-milestone/SKILL.md` and independently
> review the completed milestone according to that skill.
>
> Do not treat the implementation, tests, handover, or traceability matrix as
> proof of correctness. Compare them independently against the authoritative
> requirements and evaluation activities, run complete container verification,
> and record concrete findings and follow-up work.
