# Verify an in-progress change

## Prerequisites

- A project change exists that needs development feedback or diagnosis.
- The relevant milestone and authoritative specifications are available.

## Prompt

> Read `agent-framework/skills/verify-change/SKILL.md` and verify the current
> change according to that skill.
>
> Start with the narrowest meaningful containerized check, diagnose failures at
> the correct layer, and widen verification as appropriate. Do not weaken tests,
> contracts, or verification policy merely to make the check pass.
